      Abstract: 
          This 2021 Population Census dataset contains statistics relevant to demographic, household, educational, 
          economic, housing and internal migration characteristics of the Hong Kong population residing in the 18 
          District Council districts in 2021. The dataset also contains the boundaries of individual District Council 
          districts. Since 1961, a population census has been conducted in Hong Kong every 10 years and a by-census in 
          the middle of the intercensal period. The 2021 Population Census, which was conducted in June to August 
          2021, provides benchmark statistics on the socio-economic characteristics of the Hong Kong population vital 
          to the planning and policy formulation of the government.
