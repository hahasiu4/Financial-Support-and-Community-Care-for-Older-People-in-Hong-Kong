      Abstract: 
          Day Care Centres/Units for the Elderly (DEs/DCUs) provide centre-based community care services, such as day 
          time care, rehabilitation exercises and social activities, to frail elderly persons aged 60 or above living 
          in the community and who are assessed and recommended for Community Care Services or Residential Care 
          Services under the Standardised Care Need Assessment Mechanism for Elderly Services, including those 
          suffering from dementia. The services aim at helping service users maintain an optimal level of functioning, 
          improve their quality of life and age in place in a familiar community. They also provide various kinds of 
          support and assistance to carers.
